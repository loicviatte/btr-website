Coaching Your Own World-Class Daughter: The Truth

with LENE JAMES MIKKELSEN

About this episode

In this episode, Marius Iepure sits down with Lene James Mikkelsen, world champion with Colin James and one of the most respected Latin coaches in the world, for an honest conversation about sacrifice, high standards and what dance asks of you when it becomes your whole life. She left university in Aarhus at 19 for London, where everyone told her that she and Colin did not fit together.

They talk about the WDSF and WDC split and the three UK leagues, her strict system built on the rumba walk, the Skills and Drills book that was never printed, and how the ETC camp grew into a camp with a live band. Then her teachers, losing her mum and pouring the grief into her dancing, raising twins Tara and Zia and coaching her own world class daughter, why a man in DanceSport is made for life while a woman must keep proving herself, spotting long term potential, solo girls and the sharing part of partnering, judging on instinct, judging Vild med Dans, and why she is not sure democracy is always the best way to run DanceSport.
