About this episode

In this episode, Marius Iepure sits down with Amanda Besyedin and Sarah Nolan, professional Latin dancers and hosts of the Ballroom Girl Talk podcast, for Break The Routine’s first conversation with two guests, recorded the day after the Royal Albert Hall. They were rivals first: Amanda danced with Nikolai, Sarah and Andre were “solo stars”, then the partners swapped, Sarah married Nikolai and Amanda married Andre, and for a few tense years they competed in the same category with the same coaches, until Amanda texted Sarah to do a podcast and hash it all out.

They talk about growing up when everything in DanceSport was “hush hush”, Sarah saving competition photos from Facebook in New Zealand and learning that what made her different was her strength, Amanda’s path from competitive gymnastics and an ABT summer intensive to a 10-lesson ballroom pack, their anonymous submissions and how to tell a Pro-Am student he has a hygiene problem, how Ballroom Girl Talk started, Amanda’s seat on the NDCA Competitors Commission and the new rule that two people from one household cannot sit on the same panel, body image and nutrition, Sarah’s studio in Los Angeles, and their advice on burnout and confidence.
