HOST & CO-FOUNDER

MARIUS
IEPURE

Marius grew up dancing in Romania and competed in Latin for the Romanian national team on the international circuit. In Germany he partnered Oti Mabuse: together they won the German national Show Dance title and finished second at the World Latin Championships. After more than 20 years on the floor, he co-runs the Oti Marius Dance Studio in Park Royal, London, opened in 2020, and leads workshops for dancers of every level across the UK.

He started Break The Routine because DanceSport rarely talks about itself in the open. He wants marking that dancers can understand and trust, safer rooms for young dancers, a sport that is easier to enter and to afford, and federations that work together instead of against each other. Each guest leaves a question for the next, so the conversation never stops at one episode.

THE TEAM

The people behind the show

Break The Routine was founded by Marius, Alexandra and Loïc, three people who live DanceSport from different sides of the floor.

CO-FOUNDER

Alexandra Lukey

Alexandra dances herself, and a show where DanceSport talks about itself in the open was close to her heart from day one. Alongside Break The Routine she is co-founding InBetween, the app that turns what your coach says in a lesson into focus points you keep working on between lessons.

CO-FOUNDER

Loïc Viatte

For Loïc, Break The Routine is a free-expression project close to his heart: a place where DanceSport can speak openly about itself. Alongside the show he is co-founding InBetween with Alexandra.
