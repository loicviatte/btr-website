About this episode

Oti Mabuse grew up dancing in a newly free South Africa, walked away from a civil engineering degree and crossed continents to become a two time Strictly champion. In this episode with her husband and host Marius Iepure, she opens up about what Germany taught her, the brutal realities girls face in DanceSport, protecting her daughter from the industry, and why African dance carries a spirituality that ballroom often forgets.

A warm, honest conversation about belonging, ambition and building something of your own.
