About this episode

In this episode of Break the Routine, Marius Iepure sits down with Michael Shelton to explore an unusual life shaped by law, theatre, and nearly 50 years around the world of DanceSport. From an unexpected introduction through Peter Maxwell to senior roles in judging, governance, and discipline, Michael reflects on how trust, fairness, and authority are built - and challenged - inside a sport that lives between artistry, politics, and procedure.

They talk about how Michael first entered the dance world, what he learned from a career as a judge and theatre producer, why DanceSport still struggles with fragmentation and control, and what fairness really means in a system where subjectivity can never fully disappear.
