About this episode

Who is responsible for protecting dancers when the systems around them fall short? In this episode, Leila Stewart and Marius Iepure confront the safeguarding questions DanceSport can no longer ignore: what happens when a young dancer raises a concern, who should handle it, and are studios equipped to keep dancers safe?

Drawing on Leila’s experience in dance and frontline ambulance work, they discuss independent reporting, child protection, first-aid training for dance teachers, and the pressures dancers face, including harmful comments about their bodies. This is a conversation about accountability and what meaningful protection should look like across DanceSport.
